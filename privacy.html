Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

The short version: we keep what's needed to run your planner, nothing more. No ads, no trackers, no selling or sharing your data. You can export everything, and delete your account and all of its data, at any time.

What we keep

  • Your account: your email address and first name, and your password (stored only as a secure hash by our sign-in provider). If you sign in with Google, Apple or Facebook, we receive your name and email from them.
  • Your planner: the tasks, lists, project notes and labels you create, with their dates, times and priorities.
  • Your settings and time zone, saved on your account so Planner works the same on every device and knows what “today” means for you.
  • Basic technical logs kept by our hosting providers (such as IP address, time and type of request), used to keep the service running and secure. Our AI connection server logs only whether a request was signed in and which app sent it, never your tasks or sign-in tokens.

We don't use analytics or advertising trackers, and we don't build profiles about you.

How we use it

Only to provide Planner to you: to show and save your plan, sign you in, sync between your devices, send the emails you ask for (sign-in links, password resets, address confirmation), and answer you when you write to us. We don't sell your data, rent it, or share it for advertising, and we don't use your tasks to train AI models.

Who helps us run Planner

  • Supabase stores your account and planner data and runs our sign-in and server functions.
  • GitHub Pages serves the website itself.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr deliver the typeface and a few open-source code libraries to your browser.

These providers process data only as needed to deliver their service to us. When you use these features, your data is also handled by:

  • Voice input: your browser's built-in speech recognition (for example Google in Chrome, or Apple in Safari).
  • AI apps you connect (Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini): they read and change your planner only after you sign in and click Allow, under their own privacy policies. You can disconnect them in those apps at any time.

Cookies and storage

Planner stores your sign-in session and a few preferences in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

Keeping your data safe

All connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Every table uses row-level security, so the database itself only ever returns your own data to your account. Passwords are never stored in readable form.

Your choices and rights

  • Export: “Export backup” at the bottom of the app downloads everything as a file.
  • Correct: edit or delete any task, list, note or label in the app; change your name in Settings.
  • Delete: Settings → Account → “Delete my account” permanently removes your account and all of its data right away. Deleted tasks stay in your trash for 30 days unless you empty it. Copies in our providers' routine backups expire on their normal schedule.
  • Ask: write to hello@planner.example for a copy of your data, a correction, or anything else about your privacy. We'll answer within 30 days.

Children

Planner isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has created an account, write to us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your data, we'll update this page and its date, and tell you in the app before any important change takes effect.
