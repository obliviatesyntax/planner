Why Planner

Not another to-do app.

Most planner apps are a maze of menus, tabs and screens, and they ask "when?" the moment you add anything. Planner is deliberately simple. One screen holds your whole week, and it's built around how you really work: sort your tasks first, then do a few each day.

Organize first. Plan when you're ready.

Starting a project, planning a trip or keeping a home running means a long list of tasks, most with no date yet. Capture them into a list for each project and keep adding as you figure it out, without being forced to pick a day or time. Then each day, pick the handful you'll actually do and drag them onto your plan. Your lists hold everything; your calendar holds only what you've committed to.

How it works

Capture. Commit. Complete.

  1. 1

    Capture & organize

    Get it out of your head and into a list: one per project, trip or part of life. Keep adding as the plan takes shape. No dates needed.

  2. 2

    Commit

    Each day, pick the few you'll actually do and drag them onto the day, or onto a time on the calendar.

  3. 3

    Complete

    Tick it off. Anything unfinished waits in @overdue the next morning. Re-plan it, and the list disappears.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I have to give every task a date?

No, and most tasks shouldn't have one yet. Keep them in lists (one per project if you like) and add to them as you figure things out. When you're ready to do something, drag it onto a day or a time. That's the whole idea: organize first, plan a few each day.

Is it free?

Yes. Planning, the calendar, lists, labels and your history are free, with no trial and no card. We may add an optional Pro plan for extras later.

Where is my data stored, and who can see it?

In a secure Postgres database hosted by Supabase. Each account can only read and change its own tasks; the database itself enforces that, not just the app.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Planner runs in your browser on desktop, tablet and phone. Sign in anywhere and your plan is there.

What happens to tasks I don't finish?

The next morning they wait in an @overdue list at the top of your lists. Give each one a day or move it to another list, and @overdue disappears. Prefer them to roll straight into the next day? That's a setting.

Can I get my data out?

Any time. “Export backup” downloads all your tasks, lists and labels as a file.

Does voice input work everywhere?

It uses your browser's built-in speech recognition: Chrome, Edge and Safari. Typing works everywhere.

Adding tasks

Type in the box and press Enter, or click the mic and speak. Mix and match anything below in one line.

Days: today, tonight, tomorrow, monday…friday (or mon, tue…), next monday, nov 20. Times: 5pm, 5:30pm, 17:00, noon, 6-7pm. Length: for 45 min, for 1h. An @list wins over a day or time.

Other ways

  • + Add under a day or a list adds straight there.
  • Click an empty spot on the calendar to add a task at that time.
  • Drag tasks between lists, days, times and “Done today”.
  • Click a task to open it: rename it, add a description, set priority and labels, or delete it.
  • Unfinished tasks from past days wait in @overdue (top of your lists) until you re-plan them.
  • Press N to jump to the box, ? to open this help.
