Why Planner

The best of list apps and to-do apps.

Note and list apps are great for getting everything out of your head, but they never ask when. Calendars and to-do apps are great at when, but every task needs a date before it gets in. Planner gives you both, side by side: lists that hold everything, a calendar that holds what you'll actually do, and a quick drag from one to the other.

How it works

Capture. Commit. Complete.

Three steps, the same ones the preview above walks through.

1

Capture: just type it

Write tasks the way you'd say them. Anything without a date waits in a list (one per project, like @kitchenReno, or @triage for everything else) until you're ready. Mention a day or time and it goes straight onto your plan. Or tap the mic and say it.

↓

Power users: add a priority (p1), labels (#work) or a list (@travel) in the same line.

2

Commit: drag it where it fits

Each day, pick the few you'll actually do. Drag them from a list onto the day, or onto a time on the calendar.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Where is my data stored, and who can see it?

In a secure Postgres database hosted by Supabase. Each account can only read and change its own tasks; the database itself enforces that, not just the app.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Planner runs in your browser on desktop, tablet and phone. Sign in anywhere and your plan is there.

Which AI apps can I connect?

Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini. ChatGPT is set up on chatgpt.com and isn't on every plan; Gemini only allows it in the US for now. “How to connect” in the AI section has the steps for each.

Does voice input work everywhere?

It uses your browser's built-in speech recognition: Chrome, Edge and Safari. Typing works everywhere.

Use Planner from Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini

Connect once, then talk to your real Planner. It signs in as you, and the AI app asks before it changes anything.

1. Connect your app

Server URL https://eiwoavnrsdnffiztwziq.supabase.co/functions/v1/mcp
Claude Works
  1. On claude.ai or in the Claude desktop app: Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector.
  2. Name it Planner, paste the server URL, and under OAuth client pick Register automatically.
  3. Click Add, then Connect. Sign in to Planner if asked and click Allow.

Custom connectors may depend on your Claude plan.

ChatGPT Set up on the web
  1. On chatgpt.com, go to Plugins (chatgpt.com/plugins), click +, then Add custom MCP server.
  2. Name it Planner, paste the server URL, and choose OAuth for authentication.
  3. Accept the warning, Create and Install it, then sign in to Planner and click Allow.
  4. In a chat, type @ and pick Planner.

Not available on every ChatGPT plan or workspace: workspace admins can turn it off.

Gemini US only, for now
  1. On gemini.google.com, open More in the sidebar, then Customize → Connectors, and add a custom MCP server.
  2. Name it Planner, paste the server URL and continue. Sign in to Planner if asked and click Allow.
  3. In a chat, type @ and pick Planner. Once added, it also works in the Gemini mobile app.

Tip: Gemini answers questions like “What's my plan today?” from Google Calendar unless you say otherwise. Start with @Planner, or say “use Planner”, e.g. “Use Planner: what's on my plan today?”

Google limits custom apps to people in the US, 18 or over, signed in with a personal Google account (not work or school), using Gemini in English with History on. Elsewhere it isn't possible yet.
