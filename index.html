Adding tasks

Type in the box and press Enter, or click the mic and speak. Mix and match anything below in one line.

Where it goes

Dentist today 5pmToday's calendar at 5:00 PM
Gym fri 6-7pmFriday's calendar, 6:00 to 7:00 PM
Review doc nov 20 at 14:00 for 1hNov 20, 2:00 PM, one hour long
Call mom tomorrowTomorrow's to-do list (a day, no time)
Renew passport @weeklyThe @weekly list (created if it's new)
Buy groceries@triage (no day, time or list)

Days: today, tonight, tomorrow, monday…friday (or mon, tue…), next monday, nov 20. Times: 5pm, 5:30pm, 17:00, noon, 6-7pm. Length: for 45 min, for 1h. An @list wins over a day or time.

Priority and labels

p1 p2 p3Priority: must do, should do, nice to have
#p #workLabels, by shortcut or name; add several
Dentist tomorrow 5pm p1 #pAll together

Speaking

“… list weekly”, “… in list upcoming E”@weekly, @upcomingE
“… at weekly” (an existing list)@weekly
“… label personal”, “… tag work”#p, #w
“… priority one”, “… P 1”p1
“Call mom tomorrow at 5 p.m.”Days and times as above

The words appear in the box: check them, then press Enter. Works in Chrome, Edge and Safari.

Other ways

  • + Add under a day or a list adds straight there.
  • Click an empty spot on the calendar to add a task at that time.
  • Drag tasks between lists, days, times and “Done today”.
  • Double-click a task to rename it; p1 and #p work there too.
  • Press N to jump to the box, ? to open this help.
