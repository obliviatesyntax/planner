About

A calmer way to plan your day.

Planner is an independent product, made by people who got tired of juggling a notes app, a to-do app and a calendar. We wanted one page that holds everything we might do, and makes it easy to commit to the few things we'll actually do today. So we built it.

What we believe

One page beats ten screens

Your lists, today's plan and your calendar sit side by side. No menus to dig through, no settings maze.

Not everything needs a date

Ideas wait in a list, without made-up due dates, until you decide when they happen. Then a drag puts them on your day.

Plain words, not syntax

Type tasks the way you'd say them: “Dentist tomorrow at 5pm”. Or say them out loud. Or ask your AI assistant.

Your data is yours

Only your account can read your tasks; the database itself enforces it. Export everything any time, or delete your account and everything in it from Settings.

How Planner is run

  • Free to use. Everything you see today is free, with no ads and no trial. If we ever add a paid plan, it will be for extras, and we'll say so well in advance.
  • No ads, no selling data, no tracking. Planner has no advertising, no analytics trackers, and we never sell or share your tasks. See the privacy policy for the details.
  • Built on solid ground. Your data is stored in a managed Postgres database (Supabase), with row-level security on every table, and every connection is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Shaped by the people who use it. Planner keeps improving based on what you tell us. Tell us what would make your day easier.
